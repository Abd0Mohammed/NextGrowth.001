Common questions.

Do you work with only one industry?

No. Nex Growth is positioned as a general digital marketing agency. We adapt the plan to the business and its audience rather than limiting the service to one sector.

Is the advertising budget included in your fee?

No. The advertising budget is separate from management fees. The budget and payment setup should be agreed with the client before campaigns launch.

Can you guarantee leads or sales?

No responsible agency can guarantee a specific result in every situation. We agree on deliverables and measurement, then use campaign data to improve performance.

Do you have a portfolio or case studies?

This page does not claim past client results or display fabricated case studies. We can discuss your needs and define a suitable scope before work begins.
